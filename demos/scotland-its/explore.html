Congestion against journey time

Bubble size is traffic volume, colour is corridor. Each bubble is one site.

Journey times rise steadily with congested share, but cost rises far faster. The 14 sites above 15% congested hours average £36.3m a year each, against £10.1m for the 42 sites below 5% — roughly 3.6× the cost from the same class of asset. The driver is volume multiplied by delay, not a collapse in throughput: no site here reaches the hypercongestion threshold where flow itself starts falling. That matters for intervention design — these are demand-management sites, not capacity-failure sites.
