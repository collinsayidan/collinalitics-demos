Example work

Platforms you can actually click through.

Every project below is a working analytics platform, not a screenshot. Filter it, sort it, check the numbers against the stated method. They run on synthetic data because we can't show you our clients' figures — which means you can explore them freely, and see exactly how the work is put together.

Why synthetic data

Confidentiality holds

Client numbers stay with the client. Nothing here reveals anyone's commercial position, so nothing has to be blurred, cropped or watered down.

The method is the point

Data modelling, KPI definitions, quality rules and economic valuation are identical whether the numbers are real or generated. That is what you are buying, and that is what these show.

You can stress-test it

Every assumption is written down on each project's method page, including the limitations. Check our working — it's the fastest way to judge whether we'd be useful to you.

How this applies to your data

The same approach fits far smaller datasets. Most of our work is a few thousand rows in a handful of spreadsheets, not a hundred thousand sensor readings — but the sequence is the same: agree what the numbers mean, model the data so it refreshes without manual work, quantify what it costs or earns, and make the quality visible rather than hiding it.
