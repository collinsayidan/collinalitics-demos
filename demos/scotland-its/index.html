How it was built

Pipeline

Python generator → dbt warehouse of 33 models across bronze, staging, intermediate and marts → PostgreSQL star schema → Power BI semantic model.

Quality

Twelve explicit rules, each with a stated disposition. 120 automated tests. 95.70% of records pass and enter the analytical layer; the rest are quarantined, imputed or flagged — never silently dropped.

Economics

Valued with DfT TAG time values, DfT RAS4001 casualty values and the DESNZ central non-traded carbon price of £273/tCO₂e.
